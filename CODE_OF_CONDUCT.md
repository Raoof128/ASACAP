# Code of Conduct

ASACAP follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1. We want every contributor, reviewer, and user to feel welcome, respected, and safe.

## Our pledge

We are committed to building a collaborative community that empowers people representing different backgrounds and expertise. Harassment, discrimination, and exclusionary behaviour are never tolerated.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps create a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness when working through code reviews or operational incidents.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and seeking constructive outcomes when disagreements arise.
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback that is specific, actionable, and inclusive.
- Focusing on the best interests of the project and the wider Australian critical infrastructure community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Offensive comments, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing other people's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of meetings, demos, or digital collaboration spaces.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces (Git, issues, documentation, chat, demos) and when an individual represents the project or community in public.

## Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers at **oss@asacap.example**. Reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers will review incidents promptly, communicate outcomes, and may involve external facilitators if additional support is required.
